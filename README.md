# The Roomba King

A creature you meet in VR: the Roomba from the arcade episodes (The Roomba Ran Away, Episode 7, the Basement wall), living in Unity's 80s bedroom. Inspired by Dynamic Creatures, without the robot.

Play: https://unityeagleai.github.io/roomba/ in the Meta Quest browser. Tap LET IT HEAR YOU, then ENTER VR.

- Left stick walks, right stick turns (same as Between Signals).
- Trigger at the floor tosses a crumb; trigger at the Roomba boops it; a hand held low over it pets it.
- A/X calls it, B/Y claps. Or just talk to it.

It wanders, notices you, follows, circles and nudges your feet, keeps its distance when shy, sulks in a corner when offended (a crumb bribe works), bumps into furniture with opinions, eats socks, notes, cables and papers, goes home to its dock when its battery runs low, and now and then glitches through a wall.

Everything is drawn in code (three.js r149, Web Audio). Built by Charlie with Unity. Fonts: Press Start 2P and Silkscreen (OFL, see assets/fonts/LICENSE.txt).
